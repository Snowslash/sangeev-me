I build small tools that run in your browser, for surgical training, ward work and teaching.

Projects

  • Operation Note Generator

    Pick a procedure, fill in a few fields, and get a plain-text operation note to check and copy. Covers general surgery and trauma and orthopaedics.

  • Clinical Shift Scratchpad

    A quick list for ward jobs during a shift. It stays on your device and clears itself when you’re done.

  • AlignEd

    Keeps your teaching plans, feedback and reflections together, then exports them for your portfolio.

  • Casebook

    Turns your surgical logbook export into charts you can filter, and shows the exact entries behind every number.

  • Parallax

    Practise reading X-rays: compare two views of the same wire, then see in 3D where it really lies.

  • Glassbox

    Predict what happens, watch a recorded physiology experiment, then see what changed and why.
